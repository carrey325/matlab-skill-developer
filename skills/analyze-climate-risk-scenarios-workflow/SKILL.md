# Purpose

Analyze climate scenarios by selecting purpose and source, distinguishing transition and physical hazards, aligning horizons and regions, translating climate variables into financial risk drivers, quantifying uncertainty, identifying provider gaps, and reporting resilient limitations.

# Scope and Applicability

Climate-related scenario analysis for financial-risk, resilience, supervisory, or disclosure support; outputs remain conditional scenario results rather than forecasts.

A risk, strategy, supervisory, or disclosure owner requires climate-scenario effects translated into financial risk variables or portfolio impacts.

Do not use to predict climate outcomes, set corporate strategy, claim regulatory compliance, or treat scenario paths as ordinary forecasts.

## Exclusions

- climate forecasting
- strategy approval
- regulatory compliance certification
- automatic portfolio reallocation
- unsupported precise loss prediction

# Required Inputs

- Approved climate-analysis purpose and scenario source.
- Exposure geography, sector, technology, and vulnerability data.
- Climate variables, financial-risk translation models, and uncertainty policy.

# Professional Workflow

Decision steps are complete only when the applied rule, supporting evidence, applicability, fallback consideration, and accountable owner are recorded. Implementation, assessment, and packaging steps must retain reproducible inputs, assumptions, outputs, diagnostics, and limitations.

The implementation, technical assessment, and packaging steps share the following context:

- Inputs: Approved climate-analysis purpose and scenario source, exposure geography, sector, technology, and vulnerability data, climate variables, financial-risk translation models, and uncertainty policy.
- Outputs: Scenario and applicability record, transition and physical risk mapping, financial-risk driver and portfolio impacts, uncertainty, GAP, limitations, and reporting package.

## Determine climate-analysis purpose and authority

- Inputs: Financial-risk management, supervisory scenario, resilience, or disclosure purpose and governing framework.
- Outputs: Decision record for determine climate-analysis purpose and authority.

## Select climate scenario source and set

- Inputs: Scenario authority, narrative, variables, models, vintages, warming pathways, policy assumptions, and intended use.
- Outputs: Decision record for select climate scenario source and set.

## Separate transition and physical risk channels

- Inputs: Policy, technology, carbon price, demand, acute hazard, chronic hazard, adaptation, and exposure vulnerability.
- Outputs: Decision record for separate transition and physical risk channels.

## Align climate and financial horizons

- Inputs: Scenario dates, business planning, asset life, loan maturity, portfolio turnover, and discounting.
- Outputs: Decision record for align climate and financial horizons.

## Map sectors regions and counterparties

- Inputs: Sector taxonomy, geolocation, asset-level hazard, value chain, counterparty revenue, and missing-data proxies.
- Outputs: Decision record for map sectors regions and counterparties.

## Translate climate variables into financial risk drivers

- Inputs: Causal chain, sector response, counterparty financials, PD/LGD/EAD or market-risk link, nonlinearity, and calibration range.
- Outputs: Decision record for translate climate variables into financial risk drivers.

## Represent climate-model uncertainty

- Inputs: Scenario, integrated-assessment model, hazard, mapping, translation, adaptation, policy, and balance-sheet uncertainty.
- Outputs: Decision record for represent climate-model uncertainty.

## Determine implementation coverage and GAPs

- Inputs: Scenario ingestion, variable transformation, hazard mapping, financial translation, portfolio calculation, and uncertainty analysis.
- Outputs: Decision record for determine implementation coverage and GAPs.

## Determine climate-analysis disposition

- Inputs: Purpose fit, scenario credibility, mapping coverage, translation support, uncertainty, provider gaps, and decision materiality.
- Outputs: Decision record for determine climate-analysis disposition.

# Validation and Acceptance Criteria

Use risk-based acceptance. A completed calculation is not evidence of acceptability.

- **climate-scenario technical fitness.** Assess: select climate scenario source and set, separate transition and physical risk channels, align climate and financial horizons, map sectors regions and counterparties. Assess each dimension separately; completion or a favourable aggregate metric cannot override a material defect.
- **climate-scenario use and release boundary.** Assess: determine climate-analysis purpose and authority, determine climate-analysis disposition, provider and adjacent-workflow boundaries. Release only when professional evidence and decisions are complete; a provider GAP may remain explicit, but a professional gap cannot.

# Failure and Recovery

- Repair the affected evidence, data, method, implementation, or package and repeat professional decisions from the first affected stage.

# Stop / Escalation Conditions

- Stop and escalate instead of producing a professional conclusion.
