# Decision Rules

Apply only the rule whose conditions and applicability are established. Tool support does not select a rule.

## Determine climate-analysis purpose and authority

Use observable evidence to determine climate-analysis purpose and authority without preselecting the result.

### Inputs

- declared climate use (required): Financial-risk management, supervisory scenario, resilience, or disclosure purpose and governing framework.

### Rules

#### Financial Risk Branch

- Conditions: declared climate use equals bank financial-risk management.
- Rationale: Banks should integrate material climate drivers into traditional risk categories and stress testing over relevant horizons.
- Applies to: Climate-related scenario analysis for financial-risk, resilience, supervisory, or disclosure support; outputs remain conditional scenario results rather than forecasts..
- Excludes: climate forecasting, strategy approval.
- If information is missing: Stop this decision and obtain evidence for financial-risk management, supervisory scenario, resilience, or disclosure purpose and governing framework.; do not infer financial-risk-branch from provider availability.

- Conditions: declared climate use equals IFRS S2 resilience disclosure support.

- If information is missing: Stop this decision and obtain evidence for financial-risk management, supervisory scenario, resilience, or disclosure purpose and governing framework.; do not infer disclosure-resilience-branch from provider availability.

### Fallback

Outcome: stop and request evidence. Stop and request the missing or conflicting evidence needed to determine climate-analysis purpose and authority.
