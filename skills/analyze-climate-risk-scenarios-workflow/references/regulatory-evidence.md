# Regulatory Evidence

This human-readable map supports professional claims. Vendor examples are intentionally excluded because implementation feasibility is not professional authority.

## Determine climate-analysis purpose and authority

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *Principles for effective management and supervision of climate-related financial risks*
- Locator: section: Principles 5 12 and 15; risk measurement and scenario analysis
- Source ID: BCBS-CLIMATE-PRINCIPLES-2022
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: international; Climate-related scenario analysis for financial-risk, resilience, supervisory, or disclosure support; outputs remain conditional scenario results rather than forecasts.
- Interpretation: Banks should integrate material climate drivers into traditional risk categories and stress testing over relevant horizons.

- Source: IFRS Foundation, *IFRS S2 Climate-related Disclosures*
- Locator: section: Paragraph 22 and Appendix B climate resilience
- Source ID: IFRS-S2-CLIMATE-2023
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: ifrs-sustainability; Climate-related scenario analysis for financial-risk, resilience, supervisory, or disclosure support; outputs remain conditional scenario results rather than forecasts.
- Interpretation: IFRS S2 scenario analysis informs climate resilience disclosure and uses an approach commensurate with circumstances.
