# Regulatory Evidence

This human-readable map supports professional claims. Vendor examples are intentionally excluded because implementation feasibility is not professional authority.

## Regulatory-capital and accounting lifetime-PD purposes require separate semantics and applicability.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *The Basel Framework*
- Locator: section: CRE36.63
- Source ID: BASEL-FRAMEWORK-CURRENT
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: international; prudential regulatory capital
- Interpretation: For IRB, PD estimates are long-run averages of one-year default rates, subject to specified retail exceptions.

- Source: IFRS Foundation, *IFRS 9 Financial Instruments*
- Locator: section: About IFRS 9, impairment
- Source ID: IFRS-9
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: ifrs; financial reporting
- Interpretation: IFRS 9 includes an expected-credit-loss impairment model and distinguishes 12-month and lifetime expected credit losses.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *Guidance on credit risk and accounting for expected credit losses*
- Locator: section: Introduction and principles
- Source ID: BCBS-ECL-GUIDANCE
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: international; accounting versus prudential applicability
- Interpretation: Accounting ECL objectives and prudential regulatory-capital PD objectives are related but must not be conflated.

## Data quality, representativeness, sufficiency, and empirical grounding must be established before model acceptance.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *The Basel Framework*
- Locator: section: CRE36.33(2)
- Source ID: BASEL-FRAMEWORK-CURRENT
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: international; prudential IRB
- Interpretation: Vet model inputs for accuracy, completeness, and appropriateness for the rating assignment.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *The Basel Framework*
- Locator: section: CRE36.66
- Source ID: BASEL-FRAMEWORK-CURRENT
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: international; prudential IRB
- Interpretation: Match development exposures and lending standards to the application portfolio, use sufficient observations and history, and demonstrate out-of-sample performance.

- Source: European Banking Authority, *Guidelines on PD estimation LGD estimation and treatment of defaulted assets*
- Locator: section: paragraphs 17-34
- Source ID: EBA-PD-LGD
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: european-union; EU IRB
- Interpretation: Analyse representativeness separately for risk differentiation and risk quantification across data sources and application populations.

## Rating philosophy determines expected economic sensitivity and affects calibration and backtesting interpretation.

- Source: European Banking Authority, *Guidelines on PD estimation LGD estimation and treatment of defaulted assets*
- Locator: section: paragraphs 66-68
- Source ID: EBA-PD-LGD
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: european-union; EU IRB
- Interpretation: Define rating philosophy by how assignments respond to economic conditions and analyse resulting migration and default-rate behavior.

- Source: European Banking Authority, *Guidelines on PD estimation LGD estimation and treatment of defaulted assets*
- Locator: section: paragraphs 66-68
- Source ID: EBA-PD-LGD
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: european-union; EU IRB
- Interpretation: Backtesting design and interpretation must be consistent with the chosen rating philosophy.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *Studies on the validation of internal rating systems*
- Locator: section: rating philosophy chapter
- Source ID: BCBS-VALIDATION-WP14
- Authority: T4 (current)
- Applicability: international; internal rating validation
- Interpretation: Interpret grade migration and pooled default behavior in light of whether the rating philosophy is point-in-time or through-the-cycle.

## Validation must cover multiple qualitative and quantitative dimensions, not a single metric or completed computation.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *The Basel Framework*
- Locator: section: CRE36.33(6)
- Source ID: BASEL-FRAMEWORK-CURRENT
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: international; prudential IRB
- Interpretation: Regular validation must monitor performance and stability, review model relationships, and test outputs against outcomes.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 26-2)*
- Locator: section: Model Validation
- Source ID: FED-SR-26-2
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; US model risk management
- Interpretation: Evaluate conceptual soundness, ongoing monitoring, and outcomes analysis in light of the model's purpose and risk.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *Studies on the validation of internal rating systems*
- Locator: section: overview and validation-toolbox chapters
- Source ID: BCBS-VALIDATION-WP14
- Authority: T4 (current)
- Applicability: international; internal rating validation
- Interpretation: Validation is a collection of qualitative and quantitative processes; no single statistic is sufficient for all rating-system validation.

## Developer testing does not establish organizationally independent validation or approval.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 26-2)*
- Locator: section: Effective Challenge
- Source ID: FED-SR-26-2
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; US model risk management
- Interpretation: Effective challenge requires objective, informed assessment by parties with appropriate authority and incentives.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 26-2)*
- Locator: section: Model Validation and Governance
- Source ID: FED-SR-26-2
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; US model risk management
- Interpretation: Do not represent developer testing as independent validation when organizational objectivity and effective challenge have not been established.

- Source: European Central Bank Banking Supervision, *Guide to internal models*
- Locator: section: General topics, validation and credit risk control
- Source ID: ECB-INTERNAL-MODELS
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: european-union; ECB-supervised EU IRB
- Interpretation: The credit-risk control unit and validation function have distinct responsibilities for design, performance, and annual validation of internal estimates.

## Monitoring must address performance, stability, assumptions, limitations, and material deviations with predefined actions.

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *The Basel Framework*
- Locator: section: CRE36.126
- Source ID: BASEL-FRAMEWORK-CURRENT
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: international; prudential IRB
- Interpretation: Predefine standards for material deviations from expectations and revise estimates upward when realised values persist above expectations.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 26-2)*
- Locator: section: Ongoing Monitoring
- Source ID: FED-SR-26-2
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; US model risk management
- Interpretation: Monitor model performance, data, assumptions, limitations, and changes with escalation proportional to materiality.
