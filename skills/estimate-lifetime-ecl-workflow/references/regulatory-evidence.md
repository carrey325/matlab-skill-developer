# Regulatory Evidence

This human-readable map supports professional claims. Vendor examples are intentionally excluded because implementation feasibility is not professional authority.

## Select governing loss regime

- Source: IFRS Foundation, *IFRS 9 Financial Instruments*
- Locator: section: Section 5.5 impairment and Appendix A definitions
- Source ID: IFRS-9
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: ifrs; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: IFRS 9 requires its own impairment, staging, forward-looking, and discounting logic.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Interagency Policy Statement on Allowances for Credit Losses*
- Locator: section: Scope of CECL and allowance methodology
- Source ID: FED-CECL-POLICY
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: CECL applies lifetime expected losses to in-scope amortised-cost assets under US GAAP and must remain separate from IFRS staging.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 26-2)*
- Locator: section: Model purpose, intended use, and output interpretation
- Source ID: FED-SR-26-2
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: Internal economic-loss estimation requires a signed risk definition, horizon, scenario policy, and reporting boundary and must not be labelled IFRS 9 or CECL.

## Determine IFRS 9 staging and SICR route

- Source: IFRS Foundation, *IFRS 9 Financial Instruments*
- Locator: section: Paragraphs 5.5.3 and 5.5.9-5.5.11
- Source ID: IFRS-9
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: ifrs; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: SICR moves an IFRS 9 asset to lifetime ECL before it becomes credit-impaired.

- Source: IFRS Foundation, *IFRS 9 Financial Instruments*
- Locator: section: Paragraphs 5.5.13 and Appendix A credit-impaired definition
- Source ID: IFRS-9
- Authority: T1 (current)
- Applicability: ifrs; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: Credit-impaired assets require lifetime ECL and interest treatment consistent with the standard.

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, *Interagency Policy Statement on Allowances for Credit Losses*
- Locator: section: CECL scope and lifetime loss methodology
- Source ID: FED-CECL-POLICY
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: united-states; IFRS 9, US CECL, or explicitly defined internal economic-loss estimation with separate applicability branches.
- Interpretation: IFRS 9 staging must not be imported into CECL or internal economic-loss calculations; retain the regime-specific scope and horizon decision.
