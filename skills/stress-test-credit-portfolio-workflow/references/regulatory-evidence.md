# Regulatory Evidence

This human-readable map supports professional claims. Vendor examples are intentionally excluded because implementation feasibility is not professional authority.

## Determine stress-test purpose and authority

- Source: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, *Stress testing principles*
- Locator: section: Objectives, governance, and use of stress testing
- Source ID: BCBS-STRESS-TESTING-2018
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: international; Credit portfolio stress testing for internal risk, capital planning support, supervisory exercises, or defined scenario analysis using governed parameter and portfolio models.
- Interpretation: Internal stress tests require governance, severity rationale, coverage, and decision linkage.

- Source: European Banking Authority, *2025 EU-wide stress test methodological note*
- Locator: section: Objectives, scope, assumptions, and methodology
- Source ID: EBA-STRESS-METHODOLOGY-2025
- Authority: T2 (current)
- Applicability: european-union; Credit portfolio stress testing for internal risk, capital planning support, supervisory exercises, or defined scenario analysis using governed parameter and portfolio models.
- Interpretation: A supervisory exercise must follow its published horizon, static-balance-sheet, risk, and reporting assumptions.
