# Develop Lifetime PD Pilot Boundary

The pilot starts when the model objective, portfolio context, data lineage, and applicable regulatory purpose can be established. It covers design, preparation, candidate specification, fitting, developer validation, selection, model-package production, and monitoring-design requirements.

It excludes ECL aggregation, LGD/EAD development, deployment, loan approval, and independent regulatory approval. ECL work is an explicit adjacent handoff.

Treat generic jurisdiction as applicability-aware branching. Do not merge IFRS accounting, Basel/IRB, EU supervisory, and US supervisory claims into one universal rule.
