# Quality Gates

Required evidence dimensions must be `covered` by at least one current source; conditional weak or gap dimensions remain explicit in the generated source-gap register. Historical and superseded sources may qualify or contextualize but cannot be the sole support for a current mandatory claim.

Material decisions must expose rule inputs, conditions, outcomes, rationale, applicability, exclusions, missing-information behavior, evidence, and fallback. Validation gates must assess professional evidence and route pass, limitation, remediation, and rejection outcomes.

Coverage is reported separately for process nodes, professional decisions, decision rules, validation-gate outcomes, recovery and stop routes, and critical real-source replays. Node visitation does not establish decision coverage.

Reviewer `PASS` records require nonempty, evidence-bearing checks. Generated packages fail release on provider leakage, broken references, unsupported mandatory instructions, or internal artifact IDs.

Source gate: at least 12 sources, five independent organizations, six originating official sources, and every configured source type minimum.

Evidence gate: every mandatory atom-backed claim is traceable; no unsupported hard rules; no unresolved applicability conflict.

Workflow gate: decisions have inputs, rules, outcomes, and fallbacks; tasks have objectives, inputs, and outputs; no dangling or unreachable mandatory nodes; recovery reaches a terminal outcome.

Coverage gate: critical example trace coverage is at least 90%; major decision-branch coverage is at least 85%.

Skill gate: Agent Skills frontmatter and references validate; provider-specific leakage is zero; required capabilities are agent-neutral.

Granularity gate: every professional leaf has independent meaning, clear I/O, a testable completion condition, and a mapping or explicit gap.
