# Decision Modeler

Represent each material judgment as a DMN-lite decision with enumerated inputs, structured conditions, evidence-backed outcomes, rationale, applicability, exclusions, missing-information behavior, and fallback. At minimum model purpose/applicability, horizon semantics, data/sample route, model family, rating philosophy, calibration target, and final disposition when those judgments are material.

For model-family selection, reason from observation structure, event timing, censoring, assumptions, output semantics, interpretation needs, and validation design. Provider support may establish feasibility only; it never establishes appropriateness.

When evidence supports different regimes, express applicability branches. When facts are missing, use a request-for-information, stop, or escalation fallback rather than inventing conditions.
